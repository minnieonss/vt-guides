The products

Use them as a pair. Serum all over first, then spot cream on the lines.

Retinal Peptide Serum

Step 1. All over your face

30 mL / 1.01 fl oz, dropper bottle, whole face

1. Ingredients
Retinal 0.05% (500 ppm), 10 peptides, 10 types of hyaluronic acid, adenosine
2. What it does
Retinal and adenosine care for wrinkles. Peptides and hyaluronic acid hydrate, so there's less of the dryness, redness and itching retinal is known for
3. What you'll see
Fine lines and dehydration lines look smoother, skin looks plump and hydrated
4. How to use
Day or night. After cleansing, spread a few drops over your whole face. In the morning, finish with sunscreen

Retinal Peptide Spot Cream

Step 2. On the lines that need extra care

15 mL / 0.50 fl oz, squeeze tube, spot-on use

1. Ingredients
Retinal 0.1% (1,000 ppm), 10 peptides, 10 types of hyaluronic acid, adenosine, collagen
2. What it does
Twice the retinal of the serum for deep lines. Peptides, hyaluronic acid and collagen keep the spot hydrated, so it stays gentle
3. What you'll see
Deep lines on the forehead, smile lines and neck look smoother
4. How to use
Day or night. Last step of your routine, on the lines that need extra care. In the morning, finish with sunscreen
